        About

        What is real here

        Measured
        Positions, motions and colours of 113,000 stars; 10,000 galaxies with sizes, tilts and types; 6,400 exoplanets; orbits of the planets, 23 moons and 40,000 small bodies; positions of five interstellar probes; surface maps of planets and moons.
        Computed from physics
        Day and night, seasons, eclipses and ring shadows; atmospheric scattering; star temperatures and sizes; habitable zones; light bending around black holes; relativistic aberration and Doppler shift at speed; growth of cosmic structure; N-body dynamics in the gravity lab.
        Modelled or illustrative
        The Milky Way’s far side, galaxy close-ups, exoplanet appearances and orbit orientations, the cosmic web beyond the galaxy catalogue, and the CMB pattern. Each is marked in its details panel.

        Accuracy

        Planet positions agree with JPL Horizons to a few arcminutes (1800–2050), the Moon to 0.3°, major moons to 1–3°. Cosmology is flat ΛCDM with Planck 2018 parameters.
